## Building Kopto

I co-founded **Kopto**, a quantitative research and trading technology initiative focused on systematic strategies, machine learning and market structure.

Our work sits at the intersection of statistical modelling, data-driven research and trading systems engineering. We investigate how to separate common market factors from asset-specific behaviour, evaluate predictive signals rigorously and translate research into execution-aware systems.

**Research interests**
- Statistical arbitrage and cross-sectional modelling
- Factor models, residual dynamics and market regimes
- Machine learning for financial time series
- Optimal stopping, risk management and execution
- Market microstructure, liquidity and transaction costs
- Robust backtesting and out-of-sample validation

> **Most of my active development happens on GitLab.** This GitHub profile hosts selected public projects and academic work. Kopto's core research, implementation and production repositories are private.

## Background

My academic background is in **Statistics and Data Science at KU Leuven**, including quantitative coursework at **ENSAE Paris**. I have also worked on machine-learning methods for corporate-bond liquidity and transaction-cost estimation.

I am interested in combining rigorous statistical research with practical trading infrastructure.
